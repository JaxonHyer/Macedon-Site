A website about Ancient Macedonia from about 350 to 280 BC.

Ok, I found some other websites so here we go. 

So, Macedon took over like most of Greece and a lot of the Persian empire in like 25 years thanks to Phillip II and his army reforms. (later Alexander the Great did some conquering too)

So Phillip II was quite good at diplomacy and political maneuvers but he was responsible for reforming Macedon's armies into the great Macedonian army that took over all the area around the Aegean sea. The military trademark of Macedonia was the Macedonian Phalanx. This was an infantry formation that Phillip II invented through use of 6 meter pikes called Sarrissas.

It was basically a giant block of well - coordinated soldiers holding a Sarissa each and an Aspis shield each. It was very effective due to the coordination and strict hierarchy and the raw length of the Sarissa. 

Phillip II was a hostage in Thebes while he was young between 367 and 360 BC. So he saw some battle tactics of the general Epaminondas who was restructuring his Infantry and learned from those witnesses. 


Phillip used the Phalanx to hold off enemies while heavy infantry and cavalry attacked the enemies. This was the strategy that Macedonia used to conquer everything around it.
